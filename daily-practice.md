# Daily Practice

## Morning
- Ask: What would my 10x confident self do today?
- Set 1 bold action

## Midday
- Take the bold action (even if uncomfortable)

## Night
- What did I do well?
- Where did I hold back?
- What would my 10x self have done differently?

## Rule
Consistency over perfection.

## Expression Practice
- Speak in the moment (Gate 20)
- Share one opinion or insight (Gate 17)

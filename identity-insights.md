## Channel 20-57 – Intuitive Voice in the Now

Core Insight:
My confidence strengthens when I trust my intuitive knowing and speak from the present moment.

Gate 57:
- My body senses subtle truth quickly
- I may know something before I can explain it
- My intuition can feel quiet, sharp, or instant

Gate 20:
- My voice expresses what is alive right now
- I am not meant to over-script everything
- My truth becomes clearer when I speak it simply

When I’m aligned:
- I speak from instinct and presence
- I trust subtle inner signals
- I don’t need to over-explain myself
- My voice feels natural, clear, and alive

When I’m not aligned:
- I second-guess my intuition
- I wait until I have the perfect explanation
- I silence myself because I can’t “prove” what I sense yet

Confidence Experiment:
- Pause, feel into my body, then speak one simple truth
- Trust the first clear inner signal
- Let my voice be present, not perfect

Practice Question:
What do I already know in my body before my mind tries to explain it?
